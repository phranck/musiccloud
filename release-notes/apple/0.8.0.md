# musiccloud Apple 0.8.0 (2653)

Distribution: TestFlight for iOS and macOS.

## Changes

- Choose which streaming services musiccloud detects in Settings.
- Fixed the conversion sheet not appearing when opening the iPhone app with a copied link.
- Automatic conversion stays on the device where the link was copied.
- History entries arriving during conversion are reused instead of duplicated.

## What to test

- iPhone: Copy a supported link and open musiccloud. The sheet should stay open with the result. Check the animation when the conversion sound plays and report any pause.
- With both apps running, copy a new song link on each device. Only the copying device should convert and play a sound. After iCloud sync, there should be one new history entry. Mac conversion must also work in the background.
- Disable a service in Settings and copy one of its links. It should be ignored. Re-enable the service and copy its link again; detection should return.
